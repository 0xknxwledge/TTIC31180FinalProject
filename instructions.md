The course will conclude with a final project that is intended to provide you an opportunity for an in-depth exploration
of a specific problem or method that we have covered in the course. This may involve applying one or more of methods
discussed in class to your own research problem or another real-world machine learning problem, or simply taking a
closer look at a topic that you find particularly interesting. 

You will give a conference-like presentation that summarizes your project during exam week.

Each group must submit a final report that summarizes your work. The report should describe the
problem that you are addressing, detail the approaches that you took to address this problem, and describing your
experimental results. The report should be no longer than a standard machine learning conference paper (i.e., eight
pages excluding references). You are encouraged to typeset the report in LATEXusing the NeurIPS style files
